INTRODUCTION

UniBus Live shows every university bus live on a map, with the arrival time for each stop. A GPS tracker fixed inside the bus reports its position every 5 seconds; our server cleans the data, calculates arrival times, and pushes updates to every student's phone within seconds.

OBJECTIVES

  • Let riders see the bus and their stop's arrival time before leaving home
  • Track without depending on the driver — the fixed tracker works by itself
  • Keep the whole system affordable on a single low-cost cloud server
  • Serve every student in both English and Bangla

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Students wait at stops with no idea whether the bus left five minutes ago or is fifteen minutes away — causing missed buses, long waits and crowding. Commercial trackers are costly, operator-facing and English-only.
