INTRODUCTION

UniBus Live shows every university bus live on a map with the arrival time for each stop. A GPS tracker fixed in the bus reports every 5 seconds; our server cleans the data and pushes position + ETAs to every student's phone within seconds.

OBJECTIVES

  • See the bus & your stop's arrival time before leaving home
  • No driver dependence — the fixed tracker works by itself
  • Affordable (one low-cost server) & bilingual: English + Bangla

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Students wait at stops with no idea whether the bus left five minutes ago or is fifteen minutes away — missed buses, long waits, crowding. Commercial trackers are costly and English-only.

WHAT WE BUILT & SOLVED

  • Live map + arrival time for every stop, updated in seconds
  • Map never blank — bus visible at the depot before departure
  • Noisy GPS cleaned — impossible jumps & jitter filtered out
  • Tracker fails? The phone takes over automatically
  • Get-off alert when the bus is within 80 m of your stop
